# BPS Alternative Handbook: "times applied"

Survey of current trainees by the BPS DCP Pre-Qualification Group. Question (asked of all respondents):
"How many times did you apply for the Doctorate in Clinical Psychology (including the year you were successful)?"
Answers: Once, Twice, 3, 4, 5, 6+ times, I don't know.

`times_applied_2023_edition.csv`: per-course percentages from the 2023 edition (one PDF per course, survey run in 2022,
so respondents entered training about 2019 to 2021). 30 courses found; King's College London and Queen's Belfast
files were not found. Counts = percent x responses, rounded.

Caveats: trainees only (people who got a place); about 42% response; self-selected; recall; "6+" is open-ended.
Hull reports 100% "Once" (22 of 22), which is very unlikely: treat as a data error.

Later editions (2024 onward) publish course results in Power BI dashboards, not PDFs, so they are not extracted here.

`response_rates_2026_2027_edition.csv`: places per intake year (2023 to 2025), responses and % responded by course,
from the 2026-2027 overview booklet (supplied by the user; responses collected 1 June to 12 July 2026; 1,410 responses, 38%).
Five published totals do not equal the sum of the three years (East Anglia, Exeter, Manchester, North Thames, Southampton); kept as published and flagged in `note`.
