Your traits, one at a time

Each rate is for everyone with that one trait, 2021 to 2025 (the DEI era), with a 95% Wilson interval. The rates are not combined.

Why there is no single number

No combined chance. The data gives each trait on its own. It does not say how many people were, for example, both aged 30 to 34 and had dependants. The traits overlap, so we cannot multiply or average them without a guess we cannot test.

No "years until a place". The data does not follow the same person from year to year. The naive sum (a 22% chance each year, so about 53% within 3 tries) assumes each try is a fresh coin toss. It is not, and we cannot say by how much.

Your own chance is different. Experience, grades, interviews and the courses you choose are not in this data. They probably matter much more than any trait here.
